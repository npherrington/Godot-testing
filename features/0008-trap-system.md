# Trap System

Create trap templates and activation logic.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Trap templates (pressure plate, spike wall)
2. Trap activation when player moves adjacent
3. Visual effects on activation
4. Damage effect when triggered
5. Trap respawn after cooldown

## Out of scope

- Complex trap mechanics
- Environmental traps
- Trap disarming
- Trap combinations

## Notes

- Damage: 10-15 per trap
- Visual effect: particle system
- Cooldown: 10 turns
- Traps appear on dungeon floor
