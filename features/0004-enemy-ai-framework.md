# Enemy AI Framework

Implement basic enemy templates and turn-based AI behavior.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Enemy template scene with basic properties
2. Turn-based movement system for enemies
3. Simple attack logic with damage calculation
4. Enemy spawning in dungeon
5. Basic enemy health system

## Out of scope

- Advanced AI behaviors
- Enemy animations
- Unique monster types
- Status effects

## Notes

- Enemies should move toward player on their turn
- Attack range: adjacent tiles
- Damage: 5-10 base damage
- Health: 15-25 base health
