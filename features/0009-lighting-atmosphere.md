# Lighting & Atmosphere

Add torch lighting system and ambient effects.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Torch lighting system with radius
2. Fog of war obscuring unvisited areas
3. Ambient sound effects
4. Lighting updates with player movement
5. Atmospheric visual effects

## Out of scope

- Complex lighting effects
- Day/night cycle
- Advanced post-processing
- Dynamic weather

## Notes

- Light radius: 5-7 tiles
- Fog of war reveals explored tiles
- Ambient sounds: torch crackling, dungeon ambience
- Visual effects: smoke, torch flicker
